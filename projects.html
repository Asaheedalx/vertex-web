The Problem

Many businesses receive inquiries through multiple channels, making it difficult to track potential clients and losing valuable leads in the chaos.

The Solution

A structured lead capture system that records inquiries automatically, stores them in a central database, and notifies the business owner immediately.

The Result

Businesses can respond to potential clients faster and maintain organized, searchable inquiry records.
